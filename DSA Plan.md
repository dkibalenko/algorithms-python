### DSA Preparation — Specific Roadmap for Your Level

Since you identified this as your weakest area, here's a concrete 12-week plan executable at 1–1.5 hours/day:

#### Weeks 1–2 — Arrays and Strings (foundations)
LeetCode: Two Sum, Valid Anagram, Contains Duplicate, Best Time to Buy and Sell Stock, Maximum Subarray. These appear in virtually every junior screening.

#### Weeks 3–4 — HashMaps and Sets
LeetCode: Group Anagrams, Top K Frequent Elements, Valid Sudoku, Encode and Decode Strings. HashMap problems are the most common in Ukrainian junior interviews.

#### Weeks 5–6 — Two Pointers and Sliding Window
LeetCode: Valid Palindrome, 3Sum, Container With Most Water, Longest Substring Without Repeating Characters.

#### Weeks 7–8 — Stack and Queue
LeetCode: Valid Parentheses, Min Stack, Daily Temperatures, Car Fleet.

#### Weeks 9–10 — Binary Search
LeetCode: Binary Search, Search a 2D Matrix, Koko Eating Bananas, Find Minimum in Rotated Sorted Array.

#### Weeks 11–12 — Trees (basics only)
LeetCode: Invert Binary Tree, Maximum Depth of Binary Tree, Same Tree, Subtree of Another Tree, Level Order Traversal.

Total: ~60 problems over 12 weeks at 1 problem/day. This covers 80% of what junior Python developer technical screens in Ukraine and Czech Republic actually test. You don't need graphs, dynamic programming, or advanced algorithms for junior roles — that's mid-level territory.
